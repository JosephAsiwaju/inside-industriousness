# Methods

## Question

Do delay-worded items already published as conscientiousness items form a factor that is separable from the rest of the conscientiousness domain?

The unit of analysis is **item content inside conscientiousness**, not a validated procrastination inventory.

## Sample

SAPA-Project web volunteers, 1 January 2020 to 31 December 2020, *N* = 200,149 (Condon, 2024, Harvard Dataverse doi:10.7910/DVN/YOEEDQ). Planned missingness. Pairwise complete *N* for the twelve items ranges from about 79,000 to 117,000. No country field, no standalone procrastination scale, no external outcome.

## Items

| Cluster | Items |
|---|---|
| Delay | Start tasks right away; Find it difficult to get down to work; Need a push to get started |
| Industriousness | Work hard; Neglect my duties |
| Duty | Try to follow the rules |
| Order | Am not bothered by disorder; Often forget to put things back; Want every detail taken care of |
| Standards | Continue until everything is perfect; Dislike imperfect work; Set high standards |

Five items reverse-keyed. Delay composite requires at least two of three items. Three items is thin.

## Missing data

Pairwise deletion. Planned missingness is by design.

## Structure

PCA on the 12 x 12 pairwise correlation matrix. Varimax and promax. Varimax makes separation easier; failure to separate is conservative. Exploratory, not CFA.

## Non-claims

No PPS/IPS. No causal claim. No cross-national invariance.
