# Inside Industriousness — one-page brief

**Question.** Do delay-worded conscientiousness items separate from the rest of that domain?
**Answer.** No. They sit inside industriousness.

SAPA 2020. N = 200,149. Delay vs remaining C r = .42. Delay vs industriousness r = .57. HTMT delay–industriousness = .92. OOS R² 36% vs 12%.

Varimax makes separation easier. Failure to separate is conservative.

No PPS/IPS in the file. Three-item composite. Self-report volunteer sample.

Data: Condon (2024), doi:10.7910/DVN/YOEEDQ
